\paragraph{The non-associativity boundary} The intended language can reject a chain such as $a\leftrightarrow b\leftrightarrow c$ unless it is parenthesized.
Larkita has no non-associativity declaration.
Consequently this edition factors the equivalence production to admit at most one unparenthesized equivalence at each formula level.
The grammar, rather than an unsupported declaration assumption, enforces the language.

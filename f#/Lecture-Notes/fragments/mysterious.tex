\subsection{Mysterious reduce-reduce conflicts}
Merging two canonical LR states with the same LR(0) core unions their lookahead sets.
For the grammar below, the unmerged states separate the reductions by the lookahead symbols $y$ and $z$.
After merging, both productions can reduce on either lookahead, producing two reduce-reduce conflicts.
@@snippet:lalr-mysterious@@

The merged state has the form
\[
 \{a\rightarrow X\bullet:\{y,z\},\quad b\rightarrow X\bullet:\{y,z\}\}.
\]
This is an LALR restriction rather than an ambiguity introduced by the F\# AST representation.
The FsYacc build reports the conflicts for this actual grammar.
